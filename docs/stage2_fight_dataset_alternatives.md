**Purpose:** identify video-level/temporal fight or violence datasets that may be usable for this educational project. Stage 2 remains temporal classification; `fight` remains outside Stage 1. This is a source/terms screening, not legal advice, approval, or a download authorization.

## Direct Fight-labeled datasets (focused update)

The Stage 2 target is **Fight**, not generic violence. **Bus Violence is not a direct Fight-labeled dataset**: its released labels are `violence` / `non-violence` and the positive category contains more than fighting. Do not map every Bus Violence positive to Fight. It could only be auxiliary material after clip-level relabeling, which is outside this direct-Fight shortlist. [Official Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/7044203)

**Historical terms note:** the review below recorded the visible access/terms information for each candidate as of 27 September. SCFD was subsequently selected and used for the owner-authorized educational pilot. The notes below are not an active approval gate or a request for additional owner action.

## Bottom line

For the **Fight** target, the most relevant options are NTU CCTV-Fights (CCTV-domain and permission-gated), VFD-2000 (multi-scene direct fight/non-fight labels, media rights unclear), UBI-Fights, and SCFD. No reviewed direct-Fight video dataset is verified as immediately reusable under an explicit permissive media license. Bus Violence is licensed CC BY-NC 4.0, but its released labels are generic `violence` / `non-violence`; it is not a direct Fight-labeled set and must not be treated as one. [NTU terms](https://rose1.ntu.edu.sg/dataset/cctvFights/), [VFD-2000 authors' repository](https://github.com/Hepta-Col/VideoFightDetection), [UBI-Fights authors' page](https://socia-lab.di.ubi.pt/EventDetection/), [SCFD authors' repo](https://github.com/sayibet/fight-detection-surv-dataset), [Bus Violence Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/7044203)

**Bus Violence** and **AIRTLab** may only serve as auxiliary/negative examples after clip-level review and relabeling; neither should be presented as a direct Fight set based on its generic violence labels. Their release/use conditions still need to be recorded separately from the Stage 2 Fight source decision. [Bus Violence Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/7044203), [AIRTLab original repository](https://github.com/airtlab/A-Dataset-for-Automatic-Violence-Detection-in-Videos)

**NTU CCTV-Fights** is the strongest domain match (280 CCTV videos among 1,000 total, with temporal fight segments), and its terms state free academic/non-commercial research access. It requires registration and approval, and expressly bars redistribution and creating/deriving a new dataset without ROSE Lab's express permission. The published terms do not clearly say whether training and retaining model weights is permitted under those restrictions; ask the lab before using it for training. [NTU dataset page and terms](https://rose1.ntu.edu.sg/dataset/cctvFights/)

At the time of this review, no candidate was confirmed as a no-questions, permissively licensed, real-CCTV training set. “Publicly downloadable,” “free for academic research,” and “permission to train/modify/redistribute” are separate questions. The later SCFD pilot moved Stage 2 into pilot training/evaluation only; it did not resolve these rights questions.

### Other lower-priority option

**InterDigital Violent Scenes Dataset (VSD)** offers frame-level violence/fight segments, including 86 short YouTube clips identified by the organizer as Creative Commons Attribution 3.0, but the overall agreement restricts the dataset to scientific research, forbids redistribution of the 2012 package, and has separate modification restrictions. The broader benchmark is movie/web-video material rather than CCTV; it is a research benchmark, not a plug-and-play CCTV fight dataset. [Organizer's terms/download page](https://www.interdigital.com/data_sets/violent-scenes-dataset) Do not use outside its accepted agreement; use the per-video YouTube metadata/source license where applicable.

## Suggested next step

1. For CCTV-domain relevance, request NTU CCTV-Fights access and written terms for academic training, Colab upload, retained weights, team/instructor sharing, and derived artifacts.
2. For more immediately accessible direct-Fight candidates, ask the VFD-2000 and TNUE authors for written media-use permission and clarify source provenance; public Drive links alone are not reuse permission.
3. **Current status:** SCFD pilot QA/split/training/evaluation are complete; Stage 2 integration is pending. Bus Violence or AIRTLab generic violence labels must not be treated as Fight without clip-level relabeling.
